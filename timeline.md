## Controlled Workstation Lock

A benign workstation lock was triggered:

```powershell
rundll32.exe user32.dll,LockWorkStation
```

The workstation was then unlocked normally using the existing account credentials.

This provided controlled authentication-related activity without generating artificial failed passwords.
